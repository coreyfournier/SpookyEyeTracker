How it works

You'll walk to about six spots in the yard. At each one the cameras record where they see you, then this page tells you where to go next (it talks, so you can pocket the phone). Afterwards the tracker works out exactly where each camera is and how it's pointed, so both cameras agree on where people are. Have the yard to yourself while you do it.
