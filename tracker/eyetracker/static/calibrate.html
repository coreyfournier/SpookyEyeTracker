Arrows move the eye as you see it, facing it. Stand still with your eyes level, then nudge the eye until it looks right at you. If an arrow moves the eye the wrong way, its servo direction is reversed: flip the sign of us per deg on the main UI's Eyes tab (servo setup), then Save & Push.
